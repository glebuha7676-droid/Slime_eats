# candy-near

Mode: built-in imagegen; transparent background.
References: assets/backgrounds/world-2-ice-v2.webp; assets/ui/world-terminal/preview-candy-v4.webp

Use case: layered background asset for a portrait 2D children's slime mining game with infinite downward movement. Reference image 1 is the successful WINTER cave DEPTH/COMPOSITION example only: open vertical abyss, broad distant silhouettes and side ledges, calm center, clean polished simple 2D cartoon painting. Reference image 2 is this active world's approved palette/material/style, not a UI screenshot. Use the SAME clear hand-painted 2D casual game style, chunky rounded natural forms, broad soft shading, matte colors, restrained highlights. This is a NONINTERACTIVE BACKGROUND behind brighter playable blocks and the slime. Low-to-medium contrast, no bright central object, no UI, text, icons, characters or gameplay tile grid. Portrait 2:3 composition intended to scroll vertically with no horizon, no floor, no ceiling arch closing the shaft, no doorway, no single unique focal landmark. Continue the vertical rock/column rhythm naturally beyond both top and bottom so vertical mirrored repetition is unobtrusive. Avoid details that obviously point down/up, obvious drips, hanging ropes, gravity-specific machinery, bridges, rails or lamps. No realistic granular texture, no crowded tiny details, no cinematic bloom. CANDY FACTORY CAVERN: muted plum/lilac and dark chocolate shadows with broad soft biscuit/caramel and berry-pink candy formations along sides. A few big smooth candy/wafer columns suggest the factory world without clutter, pipes or machine city. Pink accents are soft and desaturated behind gameplay, no neon, no bright white frosting. LAYER: NEARER BACKGROUND SIDE WALLS, with TRUE ALPHA TRANSPARENCY. Generate only two irregular vertical sets of chunky side rock/candy ledges: left occupies roughly the outer 20–30 percent of width, right the outer 20–30 percent. A few uneven medium-size protrusions reach slightly toward the center, but the central at least 45 percent remains completely transparent for the distant abyss layer. The gaps around and between the side formations are transparent, NOT filled with a dark painted cave backdrop. No floor or crossbar connecting the left and right. Make the walls feel continuous beyond top and bottom, irregular staggered silhouettes rather than a decorative frame, no horizontal border. Shapes darker/subdued compared to bright gameplay tiles, gently defined bevels and just a few material accents. This is one combined full portrait canvas containing BOTH matching side walls at their correct positions, not an atlas or two separate frames. Crucial: actual alpha background everywhere there is no wall, especially the entire central open shaft. No drawn fake checkerboard, no black backdrop, no white backdrop. Clean transparency and clean antialiased boundaries.
