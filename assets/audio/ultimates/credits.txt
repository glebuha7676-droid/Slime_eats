phoenix-embers.wav and phoenix-dive.wav remaster the existing Soundbox / Stable Audio Phoenix cues in this folder, with original ember and airflow layers.
Blizzard cues use Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), Kenney Interface Sounds (CC0, https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) and original wind textures.
Thunder cues use original modulated waveforms, filtered noise and layered transients.
Current elemental mix: fireplace by AntumDeluge, CC0 (https://opengameart.org/content/fire-crackling); wind by remaxim, CC0 (https://opengameart.org/content/short-wind-sound); Tesla generator recordings by Brian MacIntosh, CC0 (https://opengameart.org/content/electricity-sound-effects-0); Cornflour Snow by DaleT92, CC0 (https://freesound.org/people/DaleT92/sounds/244103/); soft impact by Kenney, CC0 (https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds). Samples trimmed, filtered, resampled, enveloped and layered. Phoenix uses independent Soundbox summon, voice and flight takes (ignite-a, cry-a, flight-a).
Cartoon mix v4: original synthesized bird whistles, bubble pops, spring zaps and air sweeps. Percussion and rustles adapted from Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0), https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds.
