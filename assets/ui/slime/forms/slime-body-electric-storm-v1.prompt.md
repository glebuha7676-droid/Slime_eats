## Полный промпт

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production transparent character BODY sprite for a children's 2D slime game.
Primary request: create a NEW third evolution of the electric slime, "Lord of the Storm". Image 1 is the exact rounded pear-shaped body silhouette and rendering-style reference. Image 2 is the previous electric stage, for evolutionary continuity only. This third form must feel powerful, cool and clearly more evolved, while still cute, round and unmistakably the same slime.
Design: a full, plump, volumetric blue/cyan gelatinous slime body with a stormy royal-blue outer edge and luminous turquoise center. Sculpted soft storm-cloud tufts wrap the upper shoulders and crown area, integrated into the body, not a separate huge cloud. An elegant, compact three-point crown made of chunky golden-yellow lightning bolts rises from the head and is fused with the cloud crest. Two clean angular yellow/cyan lightning markings descend along the far sides of the body, leaving the middle unobstructed for the game's animated face. Rounded bottom, soft dimensional shading, clean readable dark teal outline. Match the bold clean polished hand-painted casual mobile-game artwork in the references. Broad simple shapes, restrained highlights; only a few large details.
Composition: one body centered, straight-on, upright, broad rounded lower body, compact head tip/crown, not flattened. Fill roughly 84% of a square canvas, full body entirely visible with clear transparent margins. Bottom has normal shaded blue gel, no pale horizontal stripe or separate ring.
CRITICAL: BODY ONLY. Absolutely no eyes, no pupils, no mouth, no eyebrows, no face, no cheek marks. Keep the central front 55% of the body a calm unmarked smooth turquoise surface because animated eyes and mouth will be overlaid in code. No hands, feet, humanoid torso, helmet, armor, staff or wings. No detached sparks or particles outside the silhouette, no environment, no pedestal, no floor, no cast shadow, no text, no watermark. Genuine transparent background. Do not copy the old second-stage sprite; design the new recognizable storm-lord evolution.
