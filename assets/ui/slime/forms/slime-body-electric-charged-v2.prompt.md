## Полный промпт

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: transparent 2D slime BODY sprite for an existing children's mobile game.
Primary request: redesign the SECOND electric evolution as a clear middle stage between reference image 1 (FIRST evolution, mostly green ordinary slime with little sparks) and reference image 2 (FINAL evolution, Storm Lord with a lightning crown and blue storm-cloud curls). These are STYLE AND PROGRESSION references, not two characters to include. Output ONE new intermediate body.
Design: preserve the first-stage upright plump rounded pear/drop silhouette, full rounded belly, original green/lime center and soft turquoise lower shading. Its surface is STARTING to acquire storm features: two small sculpted blue-cyan cloud curls hugging the upper shoulders, a modest curved cloud tuft near the small natural head tip, and a few short golden-yellow lightning markings beginning to run down the far sides. Cloud details should cover only about 15 percent of the silhouette; electric markings modest and readable. More evolved than stage I, clearly less transformed than the Storm Lord. Body primarily GREEN/turquoise, not all royal blue. No large crown, no three pointed crown, no giant lightning spike on top. Bottom is rounded normal shaded gel, no horizontal light band.
Style: match the references exactly: clean bold dark teal outline, broad dimensional smooth hand-painted shading, polished cute casual-game artwork. Same clear juicy volume, no flat vector look, no realism, no excessive shine or extra ornaments.
Composition: single front-facing body centered on square transparent canvas, full silhouette visible with transparent padding. Similar width and height to the ordinary first-stage body, not squashed.
CRITICAL: BODY ONLY, no eyes, pupils, mouth, eyebrows, cheeks or face whatsoever. Central front 55 percent must stay an uncluttered smooth lime/turquoise surface, for separately animated eyes and mouth in the game. No arms, legs, accessories, weapons, floor, pedestal, cast shadow, background, text, watermark or detached spark particles. Genuine transparency.
