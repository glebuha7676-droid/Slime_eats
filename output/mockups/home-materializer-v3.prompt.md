## Промпт

Use case: creative game prop + functional UI mockup COMPONENT. Invent a recognisable FOOD MATERIALIZER device for this existing cartoon slime game. Generate ONLY the middle replacement machine component, not the full game screen. First reference is the current home screen/style and size context. The previous middle rectangular info panel must become an actual imaginative laboratory appliance. Second reference is the red flaming pepper food to materialize.
Canvas a WIDE LOW rectangle, width:height approximately 1.95:1 (880x450 design proportions). Everything must fit inside this compact footprint. Full opaque calm blue laboratory background at canvas edges, no transparent margins. No part of the slime, room platform, world card or play button in this image.
CONCEPT: one weighty softly rounded low oval/rounded-trapezoid chassis, pale icy-blue satin metal, dark teal outlines, front view slight top-down, polished hand-painted mobile game style EXACTLY like the references. A distinctive C-SHAPED SCANNING ARCH rises from the left rear of the chassis and curves partly over a small shallow serving disk. It is an open materialization field, easy to grab the food through, NOT a closed container. The chunky arch has 3 small inset cyan emitter strips directed toward its center. A few fine cyan light particles collect above the disk, and the reference's red pepper is forming there, lower tip slightly dissolving into tiny glowing particles. The pepper stays the same graphic identity, medium sized, no excessive bloom, about 140px tall at 880px-wide display. The arch and disk are mechanically integrated into the chassis with one short support. No tubes feeding three old machines, no faucet, no funnel, no tank. Clearly a small delightful machine that PRINTS/ASSEMBLES food out of energy on demand, not a mere plate of food.
