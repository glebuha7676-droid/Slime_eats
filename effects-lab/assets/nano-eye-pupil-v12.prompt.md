# nanoEyePupil

Mode: built-in imagegen, transparent background.
References: effects-lab/assets/nano-eye-closed-v11.webp

Reference is the existing closed red cybernetic slime eye. Generate ONLY its SEPARATE MOVABLE PUPIL/LENS CORE sprite for layering onto that eye. One round glossy red-black robotic iris/lens core, matching reference center: smooth near-black burgundy pupil center with a deep ruby-red glow ring, a few simple short curved bright red sensor marks around the iris edge, and two clean white cartoon highlights at upper-left and lower-right. Preserve its friendly round cartoon look and smooth bold outlines. NO silver eye frame, NO outer socket, NO side indicator, NO eye surround, NO sclera, NO body. The result is just a small solid CLOSED glossy round red lens/pupil disk; no hole, tunnel, aperture shutter or visible opening. Isolated single round pupil on actual transparent alpha background, front-facing and centered, no cast shadow, no text, no other elements. Square canvas with comfortable transparent padding, full circular core centered for gaze movement. Same polished simple 2D children's game art and exact red/black palette as reference.
